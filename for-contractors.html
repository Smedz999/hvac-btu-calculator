Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How do I receive leads?
You'll get an instant email with the customer's name, contact details, BTU requirements, and postcode. You can also view all your leads in your contractor dashboard.
What areas do you cover?
All of the UK. When you register, you set your service postcode. You'll only receive leads from your coverage area.
How many companies get the same lead?
Maximum 3. Less competition means higher conversion rates compared to platforms that send leads to 10+ contractors.
Do credits expire?
No. Your credits never expire. Use them whenever you need leads — no time pressure.
What if a lead is bad quality?
If a lead has incorrect contact details or isn't a genuine enquiry, contact us within 24 hours and we'll credit your account.
Can I cancel anytime?
There's nothing to cancel. No subscriptions. You buy credits as you need them. That's it.
