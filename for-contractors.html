Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive leads?
You'll receive an instant email notification with the customer's contact details, BTU requirements, and location. You can also view all your leads in your dashboard.
What areas do you cover?
We currently cover all of the UK. When you register, you set your service area by postcode. You'll only receive leads from within that radius.
How many companies get the same lead?
Maximum 3 companies per lead. This means less competition and higher conversion rates compared to other platforms that send leads to 10+ contractors.
What if a lead is bad quality?
We only charge for genuine enquiries. If a lead has incorrect contact details or is clearly not a real enquiry, contact us within 24 hours and we'll credit your account.
Do credits expire?
No, your credits never expire. Use them whenever you need leads. There's no time pressure.
Can I cancel anytime?
There's nothing to cancel! We don't have subscriptions. You simply buy credits as you need them. No commitment, no hassle.
