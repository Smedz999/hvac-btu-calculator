Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2024

1. Who We Are

ACConnx is operated by HJS Contracts Ltd (trading as ACConnx). We connect homeowners with local air conditioning installers.

2. What Data We Collect

For customers: Name, email, postcode, and BTU requirements when you request quotes.

For contractors: Company name, contact details, postcode area, and service radius.

3. How We Use Your Data

Customer data is shared with up to 3 local contractors to provide quotes. Contractor data is used to match leads by location.

4. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@acconnx.com.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies and Google Analytics. You can opt out of analytics in your browser settings.
