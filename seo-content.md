## Blog Post Ideas

### 1. "How to Calculate BTU for Your Room (2024 Guide)"
**Target:** "btu calculator", "how to calculate btu"

Outline:
- What is BTU and why it matters
- Simple calculation method
- Factors that affect BTU needs (insulation, sun exposure, etc.)
- BTU to kW conversion
- Common mistakes
- CTA: Use our free calculator

### 2. "Air Conditioning Installation Cost UK 2024"
**Target:** "ac installation cost uk", "how much to install air conditioning"

Outline:
- Average costs by room size
- Factors affecting price
- Split vs multi-split vs ducted
- Running costs
- How to get quotes
- CTA: Get 3 free quotes from local installers

### 3. "Best Air Conditioners for UK Homes"
**Target:** "best air conditioner uk", "home air conditioning"

Outline:
- Top brands (Mitsubishi, Daikin, LG)
- Split vs portable vs ducted
- Energy efficiency ratings
- Noise levels
- Installation requirements
- CTA: Calculate your BTU needs first

### 4. "DIY vs Professional AC Installation"
**Target:** "install air conditioning", "ac installation"

Outline:
- What's involved in installation
- Legal requirements (F-Gas certified)
- Warranty considerations
- Cost comparison
- When to DIY vs hire pro
- CTA: Get quotes from certified installers

### 5. "5 Signs Your Room Needs Air Conditioning"
**Target:** "do i need air conditioning"

Outline:
- Excessive heat in summer
- Poor sleep quality
- Home office discomfort
- Allergy issues
- Property value increase
- CTA: Calculate if AC is right for your space
